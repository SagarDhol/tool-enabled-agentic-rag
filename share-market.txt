Document Title: Equity Trading & Risk Management Guide
Source: SM-GUIDE-2026

1. Market Basics

The share market allows investors to buy and sell ownership units (shares) of publicly listed companies.
Stock prices fluctuate based on demand, supply, company performance, economic indicators, and market sentiment.

2. Types of Investors

Retail Investors: Individuals trading with personal funds.

Institutional Investors: Large organizations such as mutual funds, banks, and hedge funds.
Institutional investors often influence price movements due to large trade volumes.

3. Order Types

Market Order: Executes immediately at the best available price.

Limit Order: Executes only at a specified price or better.

Stop-Loss Order: Triggers a sell when the price falls to a specified level to limit losses.

4. Risk Management

Investors are advised not to risk more than 2% of total capital per trade.
Stop-loss orders are recommended to protect capital during volatile market conditions.

5. Portfolio Diversification

Diversification involves spreading investments across sectors and asset types to reduce risk.
Overexposure to a single stock or sector increases portfolio volatility.

6. Trading Hours

Equity markets operate from 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM (IST) on trading days.
Orders placed outside trading hours are queued for the next session.

7. Market Volatility

Volatility increases during major events such as earnings announcements, interest rate changes, or geopolitical news.
High volatility can lead to rapid price movements in short time frames.

8. Compliance & Responsibility

Investors are responsible for understanding market risks.
Past performance does not guarantee future returns.
