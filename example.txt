TechFlow Inc. Employee Handbook - Summary

1. Work Hours and Remote Work
TechFlow Inc. operates on a flexible 40-hour work week. Employees are required to be online during core hours (10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST). We offer a "Remote-First" policy, allowing employees to work from anywhere within their country of residence.

2. Vacation and Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year. Additionally, TechFlow provides a mandatory "Unplug Week" in July where the entire company shuts down to prevent burnout.

3. Health and Wellness
Employees receive a monthly wellness stipend of $100, which can be used for gym memberships, mental health apps, or ergonomic office equipment.

4. Performance Reviews
Performance reviews occur bi-annually in January and July. We use a peer-feedback system combined with manager assessments to determine annual bonuses and promotion eligibility.

5. Security and Compliance
Internal project documents must be stored in the encrypted cloud drive. Use of public AI tools with confidential company code is strictly prohibited.
